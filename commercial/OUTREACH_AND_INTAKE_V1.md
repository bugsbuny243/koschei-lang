## 6-line customer intake

Ask the customer to answer only these:

1. **Component:** What dependency/module/worker should we examine?
2. **Intended job:** What is it legitimately supposed to do?
3. **Allowed authority:** What disk paths, origins, environment values, or processes may it legitimately access?
4. **Forbidden authority:** What must it never access?
5. **Attack concern:** What failure/compromise scenario worries you most?
6. **Sample:** Private repo access, code excerpt, reduced reproduction, or architecture description?

Do not request production secrets, private keys, seed phrases, passwords, or live credentials. Use sanitized/revocable test material only.
