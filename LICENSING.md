# Koschei Lang — Licensing and prices

Koschei Lang is **free for personal use**. Using it at work, for a client or
in a product that earns money needs a **commercial license**: you pay once,
and the license never expires. The full terms are in `LICENSE`
(Turkish: `LICENSE.tr.md`); this page summarises them.

## Prices

| License | Who | Price (one-time) |
|---|---|---|
| Personal | Learning, teaching, hobby projects, non-commercial research, trying Koschei out | **Free** |
| Individual | One named developer, commercial use | **49 USD** |

An organisation buys one Individual license for each developer who uses
Koschei commercially. Licenses are sold on Koschei Lang's store on Polar (polar.sh). Polar is the
merchant of record: it takes the payment, handles sales tax or VAT, and sends
the invoice. The free Personal download is on the same store.

## What a commercial license includes

- **Perpetual use.** Pay once; you can keep using the versions you received
  without time limit.
- **12 months of updates.** Every version released within 12 months after
  your purchase is included. For versions released later, buy a new license.
- **Your programs are yours.** Programs you write in Koschei, and the
  binaries `ks build` makes from them, belong to you. Ship and sell them
  without royalties.

## Personal or commercial?

| Use | License |
|---|---|
| Learning Koschei, coursework, a hobby project | Personal (free) |
| An open-source project you do not earn money from | Personal (free) |
| A company trying Koschei out for up to 30 days | Personal (free) |
| Work for your employer or a client | Commercial |
| A product or service that is sold or earns revenue | Commercial |
| A company using Koschei after the 30-day evaluation | Commercial |

If a project changes from personal to commercial, buy a license at that
point; you do not need one for the time before.

## Questions

- **Can I share my license?** No. An Individual license is for one named
  developer; each developer who uses Koschei commercially needs their own.
  Licenses cannot be transferred to another person or organisation.
- **Can I modify Koschei?** Yes, for your own use. You may not redistribute
  Koschei itself, modified or not.
- **Is there a warranty?** No. Koschei is provided as is; see sections 7
  and 8 of `LICENSE`.
- **What about older MIT-licensed versions?** Koschei revisions that were
  released under the MIT License stay under the MIT License.
